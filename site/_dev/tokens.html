ThemeAves · direction 1a Blueprint · token layer · not in the sitemap

Every token, both themes, ratios computed on load.

Nothing on this page is hardcoded. Each ratio below is read from the resolved value of the custom property in src/styles/tokens.css and recomputed whenever the theme changes, so editing a token without rechecking it fails here rather than in production. Body text needs 4.5:1, large text and control boundaries need 3:1.

01 · Colour · semantic, flips with theme

Roles, not colours.

A component may only ever name the role. The hex behind it is free to change and does change between themes.

02 · Colour · pinned in both themes

The plane does not flip, and neither does its text.

This is where the previous system broke. A fill flipped with the theme while the text on it stayed white, and the primary button landed at 1.05:1. A surface whose job is to carry text is pinned, and its text partner is pinned with it. A literal white here is correct, not a token violation.

One accent, spent large
The colour discipline is two moments per page, and one of them commits at least 30% of its own height.

Quiet sections carry zero accent by design. A link on the plane inverts its focus ring, because a blue ring on a blue fill is not a ring. Tab to it.

03 · Contrast · computed, not eyeballed

The measurements.

PairForegroundBackgroundRatioNeedsVerdict

The decorative rows carry no threshold on purpose. WCAG 1.4.11 governs controls and meaningful graphics, not dividers, and holding a hairline to 3:1 would turn the measurement rule into a hard border and kill the drawing-sheet read. Anything that bounds a control uses --color-line-strong instead, which is held to 3:1.

04 · Type · Archivo and IBM Plex Mono · 3 font files of 4

Archivo speaks, Plex Mono measures.

Archivo is a grotesque drawn for signage and tables: even colour, tight caps, honest at 800 without shouting. Plex Mono carries every label, spec value and coordinate, which is what makes the drawing-sheet read legible rather than decorative.

06 · Shape, depth, motion

No shadows exist in this system.

Depth is a 1px line and a one-step surface tint, nothing else. There are no shadow tokens to reach for, deliberately. If a component seems to need one, the component is wrong. Motion is 160ms linear, because a drawing instrument does not ease, and it is suppressed entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.
