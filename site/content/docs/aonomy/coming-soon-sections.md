# Coming soon sections

A coming soon page is the header chapter's page and nothing else: the contact section and the
footer. Both reuse the landing markup exactly, so the two sections are covered in their landing
chapter and referenced here for the page assembly.

---

## Contact section

The `#aonomy-contact` section is the same markup as the landing page's contact section, and the
form posts to the same `assets/form/sendmail.php` with the same
`assets/form/sendmail_config.php` settings.

![The contact section, identical to the landing page's.](/docs/aonomy/section-contact.jpg)

See the contact section in [Sections: news, subscribe, contact and footer](/docs/aonomy/sections-close)
for the markup and the PHPMailer configuration.

## Footer section

The `#aonomy-footer` footer is the same markup as the landing page's footer, map band and
copyright band included.

![The footer, identical to the landing page's.](/docs/aonomy/section-footer.jpg)

See the footer section in the same chapter for the markup and the Google Maps key note.
