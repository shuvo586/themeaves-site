# Help and support

If a question is not covered in this manual, the support centre is
[`support.themeaves.com`](https://support.themeaves.com) and the support email is
[`support@themeaves.com`](mailto:support@themeaves.com).

When you write in, include:

- The purchase code from your download page
- Which of the 24 pages you are working on
- The browser and operating system where the problem shows, and whether it shows in the other
  theme as well, if you can tell
- The change you made last, if the problem started after a change

Issues that are answered in this manual are answered fastest by the manual: the effect pairing
table in [Landing header effects](/docs/aonomy/header-effects) and the countdown note in
[Coming soon header effects](/docs/aonomy/coming-soon-header) cover most of the "my canvas is
blank" and "my countdown is wrong" questions, and the two configuration chapters cover the forms.
