ThemeAves · Section 5 design proposal · Turn 1

Three ways this studio could look, none of them a template.

Three genuinely distinct systems, not one layout in three colourways. Each states its concept, its full colour and type system with computed AA contrast ratios, its spacing, shape, signature device and motion, and renders the home hero at 1440 in light and dark. Every ratio below was measured, not eyeballed. Nothing is built past these heroes — pick one and I build the token layer next. Reference a direction in chat by its id: 1a Blueprint, 1b Console, 1c Press.

CONSTRAINT HELD
Section 3: nothing reads as a marketplace preview. No gradients, glass, waves, blobs, carousels, isometric art, icon-circle trios, or the marketplace default fonts.
FAILURE MODES ADDRESSED
Each direction publishes a colour discipline (5.3) that sits between the 24-tiny-moments wall and the tall-empty-plane wall, and defends its number.
HERO STORY
All three heroes tell the flagship's one story: a WhatsApp message arrives, a booking appears, on your server. Image slots are placeholders — the system has to look finished empty.
1a Blueprint cool neutral · signal blue · light-first

A precision engineering document you happen to buy software from.

Developer buyers trust things that look measured and drawn to spec. Structure is carried by a visible modular grid and hairline rules, never cards or shadows, so the page reads as a drawing sheet rather than a landing page. It looks finished with only a wordmark because the grid and the type do the work, and it stays calm while every screenshot slot is a placeholder.

01 · COLOUR — one accent

One accent, a signal blue, plus a cool-neutral ink/paper ramp. One accent because a spec sheet marks one thing at a time; a second hue would compete with the grid for the eye.

Discipline. Two colour moments per page, maximum. One is a full-bleed signal-blue band committing ≥30% of its own height with real content; the rest is ink on paper. The accent-plane blue is pinned in both themes with literal white on it, so it never inverts under its text. Quiet sections carry zero accent by design.

02 · TYPE — Archivo + IBM Plex Mono

Archivo is a grotesque drawn for signage and tables: even colour, tight caps, honest at 800 without shouting. Plex Mono carries every label, spec value and coordinate, which is what makes the drawing-sheet read legible rather than decorative. Not the marketplace grotesques (Poppins, Manrope), which read soft and consumer.

03 · GRID

8px base. 12 columns, 1200 container, 24 gutter. Section rhythm 96 / 128. The grid is visible: ticked rules with coordinate labels run the section edges.

04 · SHAPE + DEPTH

No shadows anywhere. Radius 2px, near-square. Structure is 1px lines and the grid; the one plane is the blue band. Focus ring: 2px blue offset, legible on paper and on the plane.

05 · SIGNATURE DEVICE

The measurement rule. A thin ticked line with coordinate labels frames every section, the way a drawing is dimensioned. It is the grid made visible and it is what makes the site recognisable in one glance.

06 · MOTION

Rules draw in, 160ms linear, on first scroll into view. State changes only otherwise. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no count-ups. Fully suppressed under prefers-reduced-motion.

1b Console near-black · phosphor amber · dark-first

The studio's own workbench, not a brochure about it.

Buyers are developers who live in an editor. A dark-first canvas of bordered panels with a line-number gutter says the people who made this ship code, without a single gradient or glow. It reads as a tool, which is the point: the credibility is in looking like where the work happens. Panels hold empty screenshot slots without looking unfinished because the frame is the content.

01 · COLOUR — one accent

One accent, phosphor amber, used only as a plane or a functional marker — active tab, caret, one panel header. Never as thin text, which is what keeps it AA-safe in both themes and stops it becoming confetti.

Discipline. One accent. At most three amber moments per page and one of them commits ≥25% of a band's area (a panel header, the demo-credentials strip). Amber is the same hex in both themes and always carries near-black ink, so it can never invert to a low-contrast fill. Status never rides on amber alone — it also carries a dot and a word.

02 · TYPE — JetBrains Mono + IBM Plex Sans

JetBrains Mono sets the display and every spec value — it is a real code face, so the workbench read is earned, not costumed. Plex Sans carries body copy at length, where a monospace would tire the eye. Mono headlines are the opposite of the marketplace default and immediately un-template the page.

03 · GRID

4px base. Two regions: a 56px line-number gutter and the content column, 1180 container. Panels align to a hard 8px step so borders always meet.

04 · SHAPE + DEPTH

Bordered panels, radius 6px, no drop shadows. Depth comes from a 1px line plus a one-step panel tint over the bg. Focus ring: 2px amber, which clears every surface.

05 · SIGNATURE DEVICE

The gutter rail and caret. A line-number rail runs down the start edge of every major block, and an amber block caret marks the live element. A one-line status footer closes each section with the honest facts.

06 · MOTION

The caret blinks. Panels get a 1-frame border-brighten on focus, content fades 80ms. No parallax, no odometers, no marquee. Caret holds steady under prefers-reduced-motion.

1c Press warm paper · one clay band · light-first

A studio journal — editorial confidence, set in type, not decorated.

The furthest thing from a marketplace preview is a well-set publication. Structure is carried by whitespace, hairline rules and oversized numerals — a serif display face gives the studio a voice with authority, which is exactly the trust argument the brief needs when tenure is off the table. It reads as considered, and it needs no imagery to feel complete because the typography is the composition.

01 · Colour — one accent

One saturated clay against a warm ink/paper ramp. One accent because an editorial page earns impact by spending colour rarely and large — a single band per page, not a scatter of ticks.

LIGHT

surface#FFFDF9
ink#1C1A17 · 15.8:1 / 17.1:1
muted#6B6459 · 5.3:1

DARK

muted#9A9284 · 6.1:1
clay-text#E8785A · 6.5:1
clay-planepinned · on-clay 5.6:1

Discipline. One saturated clay, spent as a single full-bleed band per page that fills 100% of its height with real content, plus the wordmark. Two colour moments maximum. The clay plane is pinned in both themes and always carries pinned off-white text at 5.6:1, so it never inverts. Everything else is warm ink on paper.

02 · Type — Newsreader + Public Sans

Newsreader is a screen-native text serif with real optical sizes — authoritative at display, comfortable in a pull-quote, and nothing like the marketplace grotesques. Public Sans (a neutral, open-licence workhorse) carries UI, labels and captions so the serif stays reserved for voice. Two families, four files, within budget.

03 · Grid

4px baseline. Single 720 measure for prose, 1120 container. Generous section rhythm at 160, so whitespace is the structure. Oversized numerals sit in the outer margin.

04 · Shape + depth

Radius 0. No cards, no shadows. Structure is hairline rules and space; the one plane is the clay band. Focus ring: 2px ink, or off-white when on the clay band.

05 · Signature device

The margin numeral and running index. Each section opens with an oversized outlined numeral in the outer margin and a short index line, the way a printed journal paginates itself. It gives the site a spine you can feel scrolling.

06 · Motion

Type-led and quiet. Rules extend under links on hover; the clay band fades up 200ms once on entry. No parallax, no scroll-jacking. Everything stills under prefers-reduced-motion.
